How the tide table works

A tide table lists the height of the water at each harbor for every hour of the day, so a skipper can plan the way out long before the boat leaves the dock.

Reading the table:

  • Rows: one for each harbor along the coast

  • Columns: one for each hour of the day

The numbers come from years of readings at each harbor, and they hold well on a calm day, though a strong wind from the sea can raise the water by more than the table says.
