The almanac lists the first frost on the harbor at the end of October in most years. Boats that winter in the water need their hoses drained before then.

The crane at the boatyard lifts boats out on the second and fourth Saturday of each month. Book a lift at least ten days ahead.

The fuel dock takes cards only from November until the spring. Diesel is cheaper before nine in the morning.

The pilot boat answers on channel twelve at any hour of the day or night. Call it before you cross the bar in fog.

The swimming float comes out of the water in September and goes back in at the end of May. The ladder stays on the pier all year.

The bait shop on the north pier closes on Tuesdays in the winter. It sells ice and coffee too.
