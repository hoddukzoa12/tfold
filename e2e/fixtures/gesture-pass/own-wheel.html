Report on the north pier

The report runs to ninety pages, and this viewer draws each of them for itself. The reader zooms it with the same gesture that zooms any page, except that the viewer takes the gesture and redraws the sheet at the new size rather than leaving it for the browser to do.

The viewer has been the harbor office's favorite part of the whole project, largely because it was the only part delivered on time. Its author moved inland shortly afterward and has not answered any letter about the second edition, which the office has decided to take as approval.
