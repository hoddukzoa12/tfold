The harbor office keeps its timetable generator on the web now, so that the two people who understand it can fix it from a boat. Both of them say it is simpler than it looks, and neither of them has ever managed to explain it to the other.

The timetable depends on the tide, the wind and the temper of the ramp, and the code says so in comments that run longer than the code. Nobody wants to tidy them, because the comments are the only place in the harbor where the reasons have been written down at all.
